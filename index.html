Mission log / 001

World Job Connect

Discover. Connect. Grow. World Job Connect is the first workshop project I wanted to share in this portfolio. Our team, Tech Titans, explored how a career platform could connect talent with opportunities beyond borders.

The problem we explored

Students, fresh graduates, and job seekers can struggle to find suitable opportunities because of limited career guidance, unclear skill requirements, and difficulty accessing employment platforms. Our poster also identifies fake job notifications, high application fees, and limited salary transparency.

Our proposed direction

A single platform for job seekers, employers, students, and freshers. The ideas in our workshop poster include verified employer information, zero candidate application fees, government and private vacancies, certificate verification, and smarter job matching. These are proposed project goals.

How we approached it

Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test. We began by understanding the needs of job seekers and employers, with trust, affordable access, career development, and global opportunities at the center.

What I learned

Through this workshop, I gained an understanding of how to create a website. I also learned to think creatively and innovatively about ideas that could be useful to many people.

Our workshop message

“Your Skills. Your Dreams. Your Global Career.”
